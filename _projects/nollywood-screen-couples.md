title: "Do Audiences Tire of Nollywood's Screen Couples?"
description: "Nollywood now releases full films on YouTube, so view counts measure real audience demand. Using the YouTube Data API, I built a pairing premium that separates a couple's joint appeal from channel reach and each actor's individual popularity, and traced it across the films of four recurring on-screen couples. Maurice Sam and Uche Montana's premium declined significantly after a strong start, while Maurice Sam and Sonia Uche have stayed about 80% above expectations across 53 films. The clearest signal is that likes per view fall as couples make more films together: enthusiasm cools before viewership does."
