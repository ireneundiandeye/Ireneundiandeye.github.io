title: "Predicting High-Volume Purchases with Statistical Machine Learning"
description: "MSc Statistical Machine Learning coursework in R predicting whether an online order line will be a bulk purchase of 10 or more units, using 540,000 transactions from a UK gift retailer. I engineered leakage-free product and customer purchase histories, grouped the train-test split and cross-validation by invoice, and compared ten models: logistic regression, a decision tree, a random forest, gradient boosting and a neural network, each with and without PCA. Gradient boosting performed best, reaching an AUC of 0.933 on held-out data, with the customer's buying history as the strongest predictor."
