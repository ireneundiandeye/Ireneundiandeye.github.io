title: "Book Platform Market Analysis with SQL"
description: "A startup planning a reading app needed to understand the book market before designing its product. Using SQL on a PostgreSQL database of 1,000 books, together with their authors, publishers, ratings and reviews, I found that 82% of titles were published after 2000, that Penguin Books leads among full-length titles, that J.K. Rowling is the highest-rated author among widely rated books, and that the most active raters write about 24 reviews each."
